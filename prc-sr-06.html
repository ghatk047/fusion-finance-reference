Supplier Performance Evaluation & Scorecarding

PRC-SR-06 Config

Description

Evaluates supplier performance against delivery, quality, cost and compliance measures, feeding results back into sourcing decisions and supplier development.

Fit / gap position

Config  Fusion supplier assessment supports scorecarding. Effort is in defining measures and sourcing performance data from operational systems.

Open question for validation

Is supplier performance formally measured today, and what data sources exist for delivery and quality performance at vessel level?

Process flow

L4 process steps 8 steps

StepNameRoleSystemInput OutputKPIDecExcPain point
1.1 Define Performance Measures by Category Procurement Manager Fusion Supplier Qualification Management Category requirements Approved measure set Measures defined for all strategic categories Y N Provisioning quality is judged subjectively by chefs and is hard to reduce to a measure
1.2 Capture Delivery Performance Data Procurement Analyst Fusion Purchasing Receipt and delivery records Delivery performance data Data captured for all deliveries N N Late receipting by vessels distorts on-time delivery statistics against the supplier unfairly
1.3 Collect Vessel Quality Feedback Fleet Supply Manager Fusion Supplier Qualification Management Vessel feedback submissions Structured quality feedback Feedback received from all vessels served N Y Vessels report quality problems informally to buyers rather than through any structured channel
2.1 Compile Supplier Scorecard Procurement Analyst Fusion Data Intelligence Performance data across measures Supplier scorecard Scorecards produced on schedule N N Weighting delivery against quality and price is contentious and shifts the ranking materially
2.2 Conduct Supplier Performance Review Procurement Manager Fusion Supplier Qualification Management Scorecard Review outcome with agreed actions Reviews held per schedule Y N Reviews become relationship meetings rather than performance conversations without hard data
3.1 Agree and Track Improvement Actions Procurement Manager Fusion Supplier Qualification Management Review outcome Improvement plan with milestones Actions closed within agreed timeframe Y Y Improvement plans lapse without follow-up and the same issues recur at the next review
3.2 Escalate Persistent Underperformance Procurement Director Fusion Procurement Repeated poor performance Escalation or exit decision Escalations resolved within one review cycle Y Y Exiting a supplier with fleet-wide coverage requires a transition plan few categories can execute quickly
3.3 Feed Performance into Sourcing Decisions Procurement Manager Fusion Sourcing Performance history Performance-informed sourcing Performance considered in all renewals N N Sourcing decisions default to price when performance data is not readily available at the point of decision

Systems, measures and risks

Systems
  • Fusion Supplier Qualification Management
  • Fusion Purchasing
  • Fusion Data Intelligence
KPIs
  • Scorecards produced for all strategic suppliers
  • Performance reviews held on schedule
  • Improvement actions closed within agreed timeframe
  • Supplier performance reflected in sourcing decisions
Risks
  • Performance data not captured at vessel level, so scorecards rely on anecdote
  • Scorecards produced but never acted upon
  • Poor performers retained because switching cost is high
  • Vessel feedback not reaching procurement systematically
